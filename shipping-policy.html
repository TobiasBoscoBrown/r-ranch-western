Shipping Policy

Processing times, shipping rates, free shipping, delivery estimates, and in-store pickup.

Order processing
We process and ship orders within 1–3 business days. Orders placed on
weekends or holidays are processed the next business day.

Shipping rates and free shipping
We offer free standard shipping on U.S. orders over $20. For orders below
that amount, shipping is calculated at checkout based on your address and
the items in your cart.

Delivery
We ship within the United States via standard carriers (USPS/UPS). After
your order ships, it typically arrives within 3–14 business days depending
on your location. You'll get tracking by email once it's on the way.

Local pickup
Free in-store pickup is available at our shop:
R Ranch Enterprises, 2910 Cleveland Blvd, Caldwell, ID 83605.
We'll email you when your order is ready to collect.

Questions or issues
If your order is delayed, lost, or arrives damaged, contact us at
rranch15@gmail.com and we'll make it right. For returns, see our 30-day
Return Policy.
