About R Ranch Enterprises

Family run, woman owned, and proud of it. Here is who we are.

R Ranch Enterprises is a family-run, woman-owned tack shop and western store in Caldwell, Idaho. You'll find us on Cleveland Blvd with new and used saddles, bridles and tack, turquoise and western jewelry, cowboy hats, purses, apparel and gifts.

Owner Melissa and the crew ride, so the help you get here is honest and hands-on. Whether it's a first saddle for a new horse, a show rig, or a pair of turquoise earrings for a night out, we point you to what actually fits you and your budget, with no upsell.

We are a real brick-and-mortar shop, not just a website. Stop in to say hello to the shop dogs, try a saddle in person with our 24-hour in-store return, or order online and pick up at the store. Folks across the Treasure Valley call us a hidden treasure and a perfect example of old Idaho hospitality, and that is exactly how we like it.
